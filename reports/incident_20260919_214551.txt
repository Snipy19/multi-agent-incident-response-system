**Incident Post‑Mortem – Multi‑Region Outage**  
*Date: 2026‑09‑19 | Duration: 4 h 23 min*  

---

### 1. Summary  
A critical, multi‑region outage impacted the entire production fleet. Key symptoms included:

- **Service failures** – database timeouts, SSL/TLS handshakes, authentication errors, and Kafka consumer lag.  
- **Infrastructure saturation** – CPU steal (40 %), memory at 98 % (OOM kills), disk at 96 % on `/var/log`, inode exhaustion on `/tmp`.  
- **Network instability** – BGP route flapping, 18 % packet loss on inter‑AZ links, DNS resolution failures (15 %).  
- **Third‑party disruptions** – Stripe, SendGrid, Twilio failures.  
- **Security alerts** – WAF spikes, certificate pinning failures, privilege escalation attempt.  

The outage propagated through Kubernetes (CrashLoopBackOff pods, NotReady nodes), storage (S3 503s, EBS latency), and application layers (NullPointerExceptions, deadlocks, circuit breakers).  

---

### 2. Root Cause  
**Primary:**  
- **Widespread resource exhaustion** (CPU, memory, disk, network) across multiple regions triggered cascading failures in services, Kubernetes, and third‑party integrations.

**Secondary contributors:**  
- Expired SSL certificates and BGP flapping amplified service instability.  
- DNS resolution failures and lack of failover exacerbated the impact.  

*Root‑cause confidence: 92 %*  

---

### 3. Recommended Fix  
1. **Region‑aware autoscaling & resource limits**  
   - Enforce CPU, memory, and disk quotas for all critical workloads.  
   - Implement autoscaling policies that trigger before saturation thresholds are reached.

2. **Circuit‑breaker layer**  
   - Wrap all third‑party calls with a circuit breaker to prevent cascading failures.

3. **Certificate & network monitoring**  
   - Automate certificate rotation (e.g., cert‑manager).  
   - Deploy BGP monitoring to detect flapping early.  
   - Configure DNS failover with a secondary authoritative server.

4. **Infrastructure hardening**  
   - Increase disk capacity and enable inode limits on `/tmp`.  
   - Optimize Kafka consumer throughput and backlog handling.  
   - Review and tighten Kubernetes node health checks and HPA metrics.

*Fix confidence: 88 %*
