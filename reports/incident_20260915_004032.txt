| Item | Details |
|------|---------|

| **Date / Time** | 2026‑09‑14 08:12 UTC – 08:18 UTC |
| **Affected Service** | checkout‑service |
| **Primary Alert** | `ERROR: Database connection timeout after 30s at checkout-service` |
| **Anomaly Detected** | Yes – timeout indicates a connectivity issue |

---

### 1. Summary  
At 08:12 UTC the checkout service began timing out while attempting to establish a new database connection. The timeout threshold of 30 seconds was exceeded, causing a cascade of failed checkout attempts and a noticeable degradation in user experience. The issue resolved automatically after 6 minutes when the database service recovered from a transient overload/partition.

---

### 2. Root Cause  
The checkout service was unable to obtain a database connection within the configured 30‑second window. Investigation revealed that the database instance was overloaded or temporarily unreachable due to a network partition. This prevented new connections from being established, causing the observed timeouts.  
**Root‑cause confidence:** 0.70

---

### 3. Recommended Fix  
1. **Connection Resilience** – Implement a retry mechanism with exponential back‑off for all database connection attempts.  
2. **Timeout Adjustment** – Increase the connection timeout from 30 s to 60 s to accommodate transient spikes.  
3. **Load Mitigation** – Scale read replicas or add a connection pool to reduce per‑request load on the primary database.  
4. **Health Checks** – Enable proactive health checks that detect network partitions and trigger alerts before timeouts occur.  

**Fix confidence:** 0.85
