### 1. Summary  
At 08:32 UTC the checkout‑service failed to establish a TCP connection to the PostgreSQL database, resulting in a 30‑second timeout error. The service was unable to process checkout requests until the issue was resolved.

---

### 2. Root Cause  
The checkout‑service could not open a TCP connection to the database within the configured 30‑second timeout. The most probable cause is a network partition or firewall rule that blocks inbound traffic to the database port (5432) from the checkout‑service subnet.  
**Root‑cause confidence:** 0.7

---

### 3. Recommended Fix  
1. **Verify Security Group / Firewall Rules** – Ensure the database’s inbound rules allow traffic on port 5432 from the checkout‑service subnet CIDR.  
2. **Check Network ACLs** – If a network ACL or host‑based firewall is present, add a rule permitting traffic from the checkout‑service CIDR to the database CIDR.  
3. **Apply Changes & Restart** – After updating the rules, restart the checkout‑service to re‑establish the connection.  

**Fix confidence:** 0.9
