**Incident Post‑Mortem – Checkout Service Outage**  
*Date: 2026‑09‑19*  

---

### 1. Summary  
At 14:32 UTC, the checkout‑service experienced a critical outage. The service failed to establish database connections, returned time‑outs, and logged authentication errors. Key symptoms included:

- **Database connection timeout** (30 s)  
- **Memory usage** at **96 %**  
- **Disk I/O latency** spiking to **≈800 ms**  
- **Network packet loss** on `eth0` at **12 %**  
- **15 authentication failures** for the `db‑reader` account in the last 2 min  

The anomaly detection system flagged the event as a system outage.

---

### 2. Root Cause  
The primary cause was **extreme memory pressure** on the checkout‑service (≈96 % heap utilization). Frequent garbage‑collection pauses stalled database connections, leading to time‑outs. Secondary contributors:

- **High disk‑I/O latency** (~800 ms) slowed data access.  
- **Network packet loss** (12 %) on `eth0` increased timeouts and retransmissions.  
- **Credential misconfiguration** for `db‑reader` caused repeated authentication failures, further aggravating connection issues.  

**Root‑cause confidence:** **0.88** (high)

---

### 3. Recommended Fix  
1. **Memory Management**  
   - Increase JVM heap to **2 GB**.  
   - Enable G1GC (`-XX:+UseG1GC`).  
   - Add a memory‑usage alert threshold at **80 %**.  

2. **Credential Rotation**  
   - Rotate the `db‑reader` credentials.  
   - Update service configuration to reference the new secret.  

3. **Network & Storage**  
   - Replace the failing NIC cable on `eth0` to eliminate packet loss.  
   - Investigate and remediate disk I/O bottlenecks (e.g., check SSD health, adjust I/O scheduler).  

**Fix confidence:** **0.85** (high)
